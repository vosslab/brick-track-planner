Drag track with a mouse, or use Move and choose a destination. Drag open space to pan. Pinch or use plus and minus to zoom. Rotate view turns the drawing clockwise. Press Enter to select a focused piece or choose a highlighted rail end. Command+A or Control+A selects all visible track. Hold Command or Control and drag to select several pieces. Arrow keys move selected track one stud. When choosing a placement on the drawing, press Enter on the drawing to place at the center of the view.
